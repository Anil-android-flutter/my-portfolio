The challenge

Bedtime temple stories needed a real product: playback on phones, content the team could manage themselves, a subscription path that could go live on stores, and a marketing site that could explain the product and convert parents — not a prototype player.

The solution

A cross-platform Flutter system: Android and iOS apps, Flutter web admin, Firebase for auth, data, media and functions, Razorpay for subscriptions, plus the marketing site at templegirl.com.

What I built

  • Flutter Android and iOS storytelling apps
  • Flutter Web admin and content management
  • Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Analytics and Cloud Functions
  • Subscription workflows and Razorpay
  • Marketing website for subscribe / listen / convert
  • Store submissions and post-launch support

Decisions that mattered

  • One Flutter codebase across consumer apps and admin — faster shipping, one team language.
  • Backend and payments treated as product surfaces, not afterthoughts after UI.
  • Website as part of the funnel: parents discover, subscribe, then open the app.

Production delivery

App Store, Google Play and web — owned from architecture through launch. Live with 40K+ downloads and 3K+ subscriptions.
