Temple Girl Kids

Bedtime stories from temples — 40K+ downloads, 3K+ subscriptions

The challenge

Bedtime temple stories needed a real product: playback on phones, content the team could manage themselves, and a subscription path that could go live on stores — not a prototype player.

The solution

A cross-platform Flutter system: Android and iOS apps, Flutter web admin, Firebase for auth, data, media and functions, Razorpay for subscriptions, plus a marketing site at templegirl.com.

What I built

  • Flutter Android and iOS storytelling apps
  • Flutter Web admin and content management
  • Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Analytics and Cloud Functions
  • Subscription workflows and Razorpay
  • Store submissions and post-launch support

Engineering highlights

One codebase across consumer apps and admin. Backend and payments treated as part of the product, not extras after the UI.

Production delivery

App Store, Google Play and web — owned from architecture through launch. Live with 40K+ downloads and 3K+ subscriptions.
